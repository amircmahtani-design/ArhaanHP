About the Chronicles

This is Arhaan's own library. Every book on the shelf is a story where he is the hero, at Mirecastle Academy and far beyond it: secret rooms, singing islands, clockwork skyships and mysteries only a clever Viridian could untangle.

Press Generate New Story and a brand-new adventure is written just for him, with its own painted cover. Sometimes his sister Alysia comes along, sometimes Uncle Tito does, and sometimes Arhaan goes it alone. Once a story is written it stays on the shelf for good, ready to be read again on any device.

Tap a book to open it, then turn the pages with the arrows, a swipe, or the arrow keys on a keyboard.
